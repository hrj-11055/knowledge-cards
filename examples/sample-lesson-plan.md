# Sample lesson plan: square of a sum

This short lesson plan was written for this repository and is freely reusable under the repository's MIT license.

## Learning objective

Explain why the square of a sum contains the middle term twice.

## Teaching note

A square with side length a+b can be partitioned into one a by a square, two a by b rectangles, and one b by b square. Its area is therefore a²+2ab+b². The same area is (a+b)². For example, a=2 and b=3 give 25 on both sides.

## Common error

Writing (a+b)²=a²+b² leaves out the areas of both a by b rectangles.
