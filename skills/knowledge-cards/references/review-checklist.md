# Review before distribution

- **Source and rights:** Each fact traces to a source; included text and visuals may be reused for the intended audience.
- **Content:** Wording, numbers, units, dates, and stated conditions match the checked ledger.
- **Formula:** Every operator, sign, subscript, superscript, and variable is correct. Substitute a simple example where possible.
- **Diagram:** Arrows, labels, scale claims, and relationships agree with the content.
- **Visual:** No missing or added words, garbled Chinese, cropped text, low contrast, or unreadable text at target size.
- **Record:** Store the final image, prompt, sources, reviewer, date, and `approved` / `needs_fix` / `rejected` status.

If any content check fails, reject the image. An automated score may prioritize review but cannot approve a card.
